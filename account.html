What an account is for

  • A name on the ladder. Ratings kept by a referee that watched the game, rather than by the two browsers playing it.
  • Your games, anywhere. They follow you between devices, and each has an address anybody can open.
  • A rating for each clock. Bullet, blitz, rapid and untimed are rated apart, because they are different games.

You do not need one

Playing a friend in a private room, playing the engine, and playing across one device all work with no account at all — and always will. What you play without one is kept in this browser.

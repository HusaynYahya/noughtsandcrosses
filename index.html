How it works

The rules in full, and what to aim for →

1 Play a square

The nine small boards sit inside one big one. You start anywhere.

2 Send them somewhere

Where your mark lands inside its small board is the board your opponent must play in next. Top right sends them to the top-right board.

3 Win three boards in a row

A won board still gets played in while it has squares left — which is how you steer somebody where you want them.

The engine taught itself

It played itself several hundred thousand times to learn which moves are worth a look, and fitted what a position is worth to fifteen thousand of its own games. Every change to it had to win a match against the version before it — several promising ones did not. When your game ends it goes back over it with you: every inaccuracy, mistake and blunder, and the line it would have played.

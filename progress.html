Nine generations of self-play

The engine plays itself, learns which moves its own search spent its time on, and keeps the result only when the new weights can beat the old ones over a match. Every number those runs produced is on this page.

Strength against the starting weights

Each generation plays generation 0 — the hand-written starting point — over 50 games, colours alternating. Filled marks were adopted by their gate; hollow marks were thrown away by it. The bars are the 95% interval on a match that short, and they are wide enough to explain why two of the discarded generations look better here than the champion: a 50-game match cannot tell these apart.

The playoff that settled it

On the 50-game ladder, two generations their own gate had thrown away looked better than the champion. Three 200-game matches were played to find out whether that was real. It was not: the champion held. The lesson is about the instrument, not the engine — a short match cannot resolve a small difference.

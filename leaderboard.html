How the rating works

Ordinary Elo, the chess one. Everybody starts at 1200. After a rated game your rating moves by K × (what you scored − what you were expected to score), where the expectation comes from the gap between the two ratings: 400 points is ten-to-one odds. K is 40 while a rating is new, 24 once it has settled, and 16 above 2100 — big steps to find your level, small ones to keep it.

Both browsers run the same arithmetic on the same two numbers, so the two sides agree without anybody being asked. Games against the engine, across one table, or by message are kept but not rated, and a game abandoned in the first few moves does not count.

Nobody can vouch for a rating here but the browser that keeps it. It is a record between people who play each other, not a ranking anybody polices.
